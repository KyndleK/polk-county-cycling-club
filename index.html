Bolivar, Missouri

Everyone benefits from getting on two wheels.

PoCo Cycling is Polk County’s community bike club — weeknight spins, country-road miles, Frisco Highline adventures, and a crew that cheers you on whether you’re clipping in for the first time or chasing century dreams.

Come ride with us Explore routes

Country roads. Big hearts.

The Country Miler is our annual gran fondo on Polk County country roads — not a rail-trail ride. Proceeds support Alpha House Pregnancy Resource Center. 60-mile and 23-mile routes, three SAG stops, tacos at the finish.
