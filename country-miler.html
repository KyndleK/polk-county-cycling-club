Country Miler 2026

Riders, roads, and start-line energy from this year’s gran fondo.

From the course

A short taste of Country Miler energy on Polk County country roads — muted clip, play when you’re ready.

Start line

Riders rolled out from First Baptist Church, Bolivar — the 60-mile / ~100k at 7:00 am and the 23-mile at 8:30 am. Quiet country roads around Polk County, club volunteers, and that special gran fondo mix of challenge and camaraderie.

Routes

The 2026 courses are still open to ride — load them up and take the Country Miler roads anytime.

Ride the 2026 courses

  • 60-mile · started 7:00 am — the full Country Miler experience for endurance riders
    Open in Strava Download GPX
  • 23-mile · started 8:30 am — a welcoming shorter option for newer riders and mixed groups
    Open in Strava Download GPX

Ride either course from the Strava route or load the GPX into your head unit. Three SAG stops kept bottles full and spirits high on event day.

Why we ride

In 2026 we raised $500 for Alpha House Pregnancy Resource Center. Every registration and sponsorship helped neighbors in our community. Riding for a cause is core to PoCo’s identity.

What made the day

  • Marked / supported courses
  • Three SAG stops with water & snacks
  • Finish-line tacos
  • A warm club welcome — not a race-only vibe

2026 recap

  • Organizer: PoCo Cycling
  • Start: First Baptist Church Bolivar
  • Distances: 60 mi & 23 mi
  • Raised: $500 for Alpha House
  • Support: 3 SAG stops
  • Finish: Tacos
  • Beneficiary: Alpha House PRC
