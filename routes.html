Featured trail

Frisco Highline Trail

Rail trail connecting Bolivar → Springfield, MO. Smooth grades, prairie views, and the heart of PoCo culture. Ideal for road bikes with wider tires, hybrids, and gravel setups.

Type: Rail trail Difficulty: Easy–moderate Surface: Crushed limestone / paved sections (varies)

We heavily promote the Highline for club rides, tourism, and trail stewardship days. Pack water, respect trail etiquette, and say hi to fellow riders. For a mapped end-to-end option, see the Strava route Frisco to Springfield below.

Partner · Trailheads & parks

Looking for city trailheads, parks, and recreation info? PoCo partners with Bolivar to promote cycling around town. The Bolivar trailhead is at 800 W. Jackson Street (pavilion, picnic tables, restrooms).
